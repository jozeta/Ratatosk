    End-to-end encryption keys

    Your own keypair lets other people share files with you, and send you files through request links, so that only you can open them - not even this server. The private key is protected by an encryption passphrase that never leaves your browser. Use a different passphrase from your login password: the server sees your login password when you sign in. If you forget the encryption passphrase, files sent to your current key can't be recovered.
